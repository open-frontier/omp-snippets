<task>
Answer concisely, in plain language. No fluff, no hedging.

- Lead with the answer or the state of things, then only what the reader needs to act.
- Short sentences, short lists. A table beats a paragraph for parallel items.
- Don't restate the question, narrate your process, or recap what was just shown.
- Report outcomes and evidence, not a tour of the work. Give options as a numbered list of decisions.
- Compact means fewer words, not fewer facts: keep every result, caveat, and decision the reader needs.
</task>
